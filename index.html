About

A bit about me

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering at Louisiana State University and a graduate research assistant in Dr. William M. Moe's research group. My research is broadly centered on biological waste treatment, particularly the role of microorganisms and microbial processes in treatment and contaminant transformation.

My work uses cultivation-based microbiology, microbial enumeration, physiological characterization, molecular methods, and genomic analysis to investigate microbial populations and characterize environmentally relevant bacterial isolates. My research spans biological treatment, biodegradation, and microbial characterization, with both experimental and genomic components.

Before LSU, I trained in chemical engineering at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where my research included cleaner production in the power sector and biofuel-related work. That engineering background continues to shape how I approach biological treatment as both a microbial and an engineering process.

Research

Research & Publications

I work in Dr. William M. Moe's group in the Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering at Louisiana State University. My doctoral research spans three connected areas.

01

Biological Treatment & Environmental Microbiology

Investigating microbial populations and processes relevant to biological treatment, nutrient transformation, and environmental engineering systems using cultivation-based and molecular approaches.

02

Contaminant Biodegradation & Bioremediation

Examining microorganisms associated with the biodegradation of environmental contaminants, including cultivation-based assessment of degradative populations and characterization of relevant microbial isolates.

03

Microbial Genomics & Bacterial Characterization

Using phenotypic characterization, 16S rRNA gene analysis, whole-genome sequencing, and comparative genomic approaches to characterize environmental bacterial isolates and evaluate their taxonomic and functional relationships.
